Focus on POCUS

Point of Care Ultrasound is an invaluable skill. From the eFAST scan in trauma to identifying AAA or cardiac tamponade, POCUS exponentially speeds up your time to diagnosis.

POCUS Pearl: Match the probe to the pathology. Use the Curvilinear (low frequency) for deep abdominal structures, and the Linear (high frequency) for superficial checks like pneumothorax or vascular access.
